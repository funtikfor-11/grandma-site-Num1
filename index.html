1. Sunday Orchard Apple Pie

Prep: 25 mins | Bake: 375°F for 50 mins | Serves: 8

Tart green apples, real salted butter, and cinnamon—without a single shortcut from the freezer aisle.

Open Grandma's Secret Note
  • 6 Granny Smith apples, peeled and sliced thin
  • 3/4 cup sugar, 2 tbsp flour, 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 2 tbsp cold butter cut into small pea-sized bits

Keep the pastry butter ice-cold and let the baked pie sit on the counter for one full hour before slicing, or the filling will run all over the plate.

2. Grandma's Famous Chicken Pot Pie

Prep: 30 mins | Bake: 400°F for 45 mins | Serves: 6

Rich, creamy filling with tender chicken and vegetables, topped with a golden, flaky crust.

Open Grandma's Secret Note
  • 2 cups cooked chicken, shredded
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables (carrots, peas, corn)
  • 1/3 cup butter, 1/3 cup flour, 1/2 tsp salt

Use homemade stock for the best flavor and brush the top crust with an egg wash for a beautiful golden finish.

3. Three-Ingredient Strawberry Jam

Prep: 20 mins | Cook: 35 mins | Makes: 4 jars

Ripe June berries cooked down the old-fashioned way with no store-bought pectin packets.

Open Grandma's Secret Note
  • 2 lbs ripe strawberries, hulled and halved
  • 1.5 lbs white sugar
  • Juice of 1 fresh lemon

Chill a small saucer in the freezer first. Spoon a drop of hot jam onto the cold plate—if the surface wrinkles when you push it with your finger, take the pot off the burner.
